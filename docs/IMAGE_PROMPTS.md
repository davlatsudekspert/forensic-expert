**Qanday ishlatiladi:** quyidagi promptlarning har birini ChatGPT’ga alohida yuboring.
Natijani **PNG** qilib yuklab oling va menga shu chatga yuboring. Men ularni ilovaga
joylayman va har birining tagiga «Illyustratsiya (AI yordamida chizilgan)» deb yozaman,
haqiqiy apparat surati deb ko‘rsatmayman.

**Har bir promptning oxiriga shu UMUMIY USLUB matnini qo‘shing:**

> Style: premium flat-3D scientific illustration, soft studio lighting, clean edges,
> subtle shadows, isometric 3/4 view. Color palette: deep navy (#0B1B2B), warm cream
> background (#F4EFE6), muted gold accents (#B08A3E), soft teal details (#2E8C86).
> Plain solid cream background, centered object, generous empty margin around it.
> No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no watermark,
