**Full description:**
FORENSIC EXPERT is an offline professional forensic reference, scientific education resource and scientific calculation tool for students and professionals in forensic medicine, toxicology, laboratory analysis and related disciplines.

- Every scientific statement shows its source and review status. Content not yet checked by a qualified expert is clearly marked.
- Methods are labelled by evidence type (international standard, guideline, published method, educational summary).
- Jurisdiction view for a small set of pilot jurisdictions with official legal sources; other countries show "No verified content" rather than borrowed law.
- Calculators with explicit formulas and limits.
- Works fully offline; no tracking, no ads.

Important: Forensic Expert is intended for professional reference, education and scientific calculation. It does not replace validated laboratory procedures, institutional protocols, applicable law or qualified professional judgment. Most scientific content is still awaiting qualified expert review and is labelled "Needs review"; no content is presented as human-verified unless it actually is.
